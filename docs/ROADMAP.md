# Roadmap — lele_auto

> Update after each significant session.

## Context
Volunteer project (unpaid) for Lele, a pastry chef. She wants a simple digital replacement for her
paper stock notebook: products + suppliers, a month split into 4 weeks, and per week and product the
stock counted, the quantity ordered and the quantity actually received, with automatic totals.
Her reference is a 6 EUR Google Sheets template (agathetemplates.fr, "Gestion de stock et inventaire").
Source material (voice notes, texts, sketch) came in Lele.zip; transcripts were done with local Whisper.

## Done
- Project scaffold created.
- Needs analysis: requirements extracted from 9 voice notes + texts + sketch (2026-09-17 → 09-24).
- Market / automation research (recipe-based deduction = "backflushing"); decided NOT to automate.
- 10 spreadsheet proposals generated in `share/out/propositions_stock/` by `tools/build_proposals.py`,
  all formulas verified with `tools/verify_proposals.py` (0 errors, sample values hand-checked).

- Second batch of 10 proposals (11-20) from a template survey (research subagent, 2026-09-25):
  storage order, cadencier, checklist, printable sheet, expiry dates, losses log, trend bars,
  weekly essentials, usage from counts, status labels. Generated by `tools/build_proposals_2.py`.
- Internal review site (Pascal only): `site/` renders each xlsx as an Excel-like HTML page
  (`tools/render_site.py`, values from the `formulas` engine, conditional formatting evaluated).
  Served on http://192.168.1.100:8147 / https://lele-auto.home/.
